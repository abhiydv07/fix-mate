## Recording Tips

1. **Use screen recording** at 1080p, 60fps
2. **Speak slowly and clearly** — pace for 150 words/min
3. **Mouse cursor highlighter** — make clicks visible
4. **Pause 1 second** between scene transitions
5. **Background music** — soft instrumental, lower during narration
6. **Test the full flow** before recording (signup → book → provider accept → complete)
7. **Have 2 accounts ready** — one customer, one provider
8. **Backup**: Record on phone if WiFi fails during evaluation
